# Privacy Policy — Video Glow

**Video Glow does not collect, store on a server, transmit, sell or share any personal data.**

- The extension reads video frames from the page you are watching, entirely inside your browser, only to draw the glow. Frames are never saved, uploaded or sent anywhere.
- Your settings (shape, blur, disabled sites, etc.) are stored locally in your browser via `chrome.storage.local`. They are not synced or transmitted.
- The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics, no telemetry, no ads and no remote code.
- The "all sites" access permission is used only to find `<video>` elements on pages and draw the glow next to them.
