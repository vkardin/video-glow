## EN
Name: Video Glow — ambient light for any video
Short (≤132): Soft ambient glow around any video on any site, also in fullscreen. Fills black bars. Shape, blur and size are fully adjustable.
Description:
Video Glow adds a soft, colour-matched light around any HTML5 video — not only on YouTube.

• Works on any site, inside iframes and in fullscreen
• Fullscreen on 16:10 / ultrawide screens: the glow fills the black bars
• Smooth fade in/out on play and pause
• Shape: rectangle, rounded, circle; size, edge softness and seamless picture-to-glow transition
• Blur, sample size, opacity, saturation, brightness, FPS
• Auto-detects baked-in black bars in movies
• Adaptive FPS and battery saver for weak laptops
• Own settings per site, separate fullscreen settings, pause behaviour (fade / stay / delay)
• Optional page dimming (cinema mode) and a quick on/off button on the video
